Frequently asked

Before you order

How long will my order take?

Stickers and ready-made spell jars are posted within 2–3 working days. Personalised artwork takes longer because it’s drawn from scratch: a single tarot card or digital portrait usually takes 5–7 days, a five or ten card set around 2 weeks, and a Major Arcana or Full Deck commission 3–6 weeks depending on the queue.

You’ll always get an honest timescale before any work begins.

What kind of photos should I send?

Three to five photos is perfect. Natural daylight, a clear view of their face, and at least one full-body shot helps enormously. Don’t worry about them being professional — the slightly blurry ones where they’re mid-zoomie often tell me more about their personality than a posed one.

Can you draw any kind of pet?

Yes. Rabbits, cats, dogs, rats, guinea pigs, birds, reptiles, horses — every companion is welcome, and they’re all drawn in the same Tails of Fate style so they sit beautifully together if you order more than one.

Can I choose which tarot cards I get?

For the five and ten card sets, yes — you pick exactly which cards you’d like from the Major Arcana when you add the order to your basket. For a single card you can either choose one yourself or ask me to match your pet to the card that suits them.

The Major Arcana and Full Deck options include the whole set, so there’s nothing to choose — just send your photos and notes.

What if my pet has passed away?

A lot of these commissions are memorial pieces, and they’re some of the most meaningful ones to make. Send whatever photos you have, however imperfect, and tell me about them. I’ll take as much care with it as you’d hope.

Can I ask for changes once I’ve seen the artwork?

Of course. You’ll see a draft before anything is finished or printed, and two rounds of small changes are included — colouring, markings, a different expression. If something bigger needs reworking we’ll talk it through.

Are the spell jars safe around pets and children?

Spell jars are decorative keepsakes, sealed with wax and not intended to be opened, eaten or burned. They contain dried botanicals and small crystal chips, so please keep them somewhere out of reach of curious paws and small hands — a shelf rather than a bedside table.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. Everything is made and posted from the UK, with tracked worldwide shipping available at checkout. Digital pieces have nothing to post — they arrive by email as a high-resolution file.

How do I pay?

Add everything you’d like to your basket and check out. Your order summary comes straight to me, and I’ll reply with a secure payment link and confirm your timescale before starting work.
