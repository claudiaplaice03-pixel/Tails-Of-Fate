How the magic happens

Three small steps between a photo on your phone and a card that looks like it was always meant to exist.

The Star? soft ears, lavender ✦
XVII The Star
  1. 1

    Send us 3–5 clear photos

    Good light, eye level, their best side. Tell us a little about who they are — the quirks, the zoomies, the way they sit in doorways.

    Phone photos are perfect

  2. 2

    We read their card

    We match your pet to the Major Arcana card that suits their spirit, then illustrate them by hand in the Tails of Fate style. You'll see a sketch before we paint.

    One free round of tweaks

  3. 3

    Receive your keepsake

    A high-resolution digital file lands in your inbox within 5–7 days. Add a giclée print, a framed card, or a full personalised deck.

    Printed on 350gsm textured card

The Major Arcana of pets

Which card is your familiar?

XVIIIThe Moon

The Moon

Intuition · Mystery · 3am zoomies

For the pet who knows something you don't, stares at empty corners and is never, ever wrong about the postman.

IIIThe Empress

The Empress

Abundance · Comfort · Lap ownership

Nurturing, regal, deeply committed to the sunny patch on the rug. Everyone in the house answers to her.

XVIIThe Star

The Star

Hope · Renewal · Quiet faith

The rescue who learned to trust again. Gentle, hopeful, a small bright thing at the end of a long day.

XIXThe Sun

The Sun

Joy · Warmth · Full-body wiggles

Pure, uncomplicated delight. Greets every stranger like an old friend and every walk like the first one.

IXThe Hermit

The Hermit

Wisdom · Solitude · Under-the-bed

The old soul. Prefers a quiet room and one chosen person. Emerges for dinner and profound eye contact.

    About

    A little bit of magic, a lot of love.

    Tails of Fate is a small, independent business creating magical keepsakes for animal lovers. It started with one rabbit, one moon and one very late night with a paintbrush.

    We believe every pet is a little bit magical — which is why we turn them into tarot cards, create meaningful portraits and offer spell kits to bring more magic into your everyday life.
